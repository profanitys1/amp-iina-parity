AMP (macOS SwiftUI/AppKit player, libmpv on CAMetalLayer) current GUI:
- Video surface mouse: single click nothing; double click nothing; no right-click menu; no scrollWheel; window drag only via transparent titlebar strip (hiddenTitleBar + fullSizeContentView, isMovableByWindowBackground false). Cursor hides with controls (2.5s, only while playing).
- Keys: Space/P/K play-pause; J/L reverse/FF (JKL shuttle); [ ] speed -/+0.5; . , frame step; F fullscreen; ⌃⌘F fullscreen; M mute (menu); T cycle timecode; I/O trim; ←/→ ±5s, ⇧ ±10s, ⌥ frame step, ⌘←/→ prev/next file; ↑/↓ volume ±5, ⌥ ±1; ⌘↑/↓ seek start/end; ⌘[ ⌘] prev/next file; ⌘S screenshot; ⌘O open; ⇧⌘O subtitle; ⌘I inspector. No Esc exits fullscreen custom handling (AppKit default). No hotkey for aspect/crop/zoom/subtitle cycle/audio track cycle/always-on-top/stop/speed reset.
- Window: default 711x400; first media load maximizes to screen.visibleFrame and drops aspect lock; later loads keep size. Optional open at source resolution. Green zoom default AppKit. No float-on-top. No Half/Normal/Double size or fit-to-screen. Title hidden.
- Controls: floating draggable card (mute, volume 0-100, back10/play/fwd10, settings, playlist, subtitle menu, fullscreen, elapsed/scrubber/duration with hover thumbnail). Auto hide 2.5s. No remaining-time toggle on click in floating card. Volume max 100 (VLC goes 125/200).
- Menus: File, View, Playback. No Audio/Video/Subtitles menus (no audio track, aspect ratio, crop, zoom, deinterlace, subtitle delay/size, audio delay menus).
- No resume playback position, no MPRemoteCommandCenter/Now Playing media keys, no OSD messages for volume/seek/mute/subtitle changes. Drag-drop files & subtitles works. Sleep prevention works.
